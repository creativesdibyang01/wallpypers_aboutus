About Wallpypers

Your screen should feel a little more like you.

Wallpypers is a wallpaper discovery platform built around something simple — helping you find wallpapers that match your interests, moods, characters, worlds, and style.

Our Story

More than just a wallpaper library.

We believe the wallpaper on your phone is a small part of how you express yourself every day.

Whether it is a favorite character, a beautiful landscape, a car you love, a game you play, or a minimal design that simply feels right — your wallpaper can say something about you without saying anything at all.

Wallpypers was created to make discovering those wallpapers easier, simpler, and more enjoyable.

What We Do

Discover. Choose. Make it yours.

Wallpypers brings wallpapers together across different interests and visual styles, making it easier to explore something new or find exactly what you are looking for.

Our goal is to keep the experience focused on the wallpaper itself — simple browsing, clean presentation, and an easy way to make your screen your own.

Keep it simple

No unnecessary complexity. Wallpypers is designed to keep discovering and using wallpapers straightforward.

A place for visual expression

Made for wallpaper lovers
