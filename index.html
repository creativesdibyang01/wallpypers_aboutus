About Wallpypers

Some wallpapers look good. Some feel like you.

Wallpypers is a wallpaper discovery platform built for people who want their screens to feel a little more personal.

Our idea

Your screen says something about you.

The wallpaper on your phone can reflect your mood, your interests, your favorite characters, or simply something you find beautiful.

We wanted to make discovering that perfect wallpaper simpler, faster, and more enjoyable.

What we do

Discover. Save. Make it yours.

Wallpypers brings wallpapers together across different moods, interests, styles, and worlds so you can find something that feels right for your screen.

Your favorite characters. Your favorite moods. Your favorite worlds — right where you see them every day.

Built with love

Wallpypers is being built with a simple goal: make finding wallpypers feel less like searching and more like discovering something you didn't know you were looking for.
